1TLDR & the story

What this analysis shows and why it matters

  • Follow depth is the strongest predictor of Community Feed engagement. Users who followed 21 or more people during the holdout window opened the feed 45x more than users who followed nobody.
  • Among users who follow at least one person, the Following feed's share of tails grew from 0.3% in Phase 1 to 7.3% by the end of the holdout window. The community stream still dominates, but the Following feed is taking a larger share week over week as follow graphs deepen.
  • Bets placed, sessions, and handle tracked near identically for both groups throughout. The follow graph adds engagement on top of core betting behavior, not instead of it.
  • Commercial guardrails held across all three phases and through the holdout window. The follow graph adds engagement without displacing core betting behavior.

The Community Feed has always been a shared stream of bets from people you don't know. It's useful but passive. This MTP tested what happens when users can build a curated feed around the bettors they actually care about.

Phase 1 gave users the mechanics: profiles, a follow button, a Following tab. Growth was slow because discovery was manual. Phase 2 added a sharing path so users could send their profile directly to friends. Phase 3 introduced the Suggested Follow Carousel, which helped surface the right accounts to users who had never followed anyone. Still, only a small share of users built meaningful follow graphs by the end of the window. But among those who did, the results are striking.

The follow depth charts are the most important output of this analysis. Users who followed 21 or more people opened the feed 45x more than users who followed nobody, and posted to it 200x more often. Feed-to-tail conversion is not flat: it jumps from 44% for non-followers to around 50% the moment a user follows even one person, then holds roughly steady. Most of the lift is in visit frequency, but that first follow also makes you a meaningfully better converter.

The cold start problem is still largely unsolved. The carousel was a meaningful step but most users still never follow anyone. The path forward is getting more users past their first follow, and then helping them build a graph deep enough to create a Following feed worth opening every day. Better suggestion signals, interest-based matching, and prompts tied to live games are all levers that compound on what's already been built here.

3The follow graph

Treatment group only. Holdout users had no access to profiles or follow functionality.

4Community betting impact

Feed-to-tail conversion, tails per community user, and where tails come from

Feed-to-tail conversion averaged 47 to 48% for both treatment and holdout across the holdout window. Both groups convert at the same rate. What drives tailing volume is how often users open the feed.

Among users who follow at least one person, the Following feed's share of tails grew from 0.3% in Phase 1 to 7.3% by the end of the holdout window. Community still accounts for the large majority of tails, but the Following feed is taking a larger share as follow graphs deepen.

6What following unlocks

Treatment group only, pooled over Aug 24 to Sep 14

Treatment users bucketed by how many people they followed during the holdout window. Users who followed nobody opened the Community Feed 9 times across the 22-day window. Users who followed 1 person: 49 times. Users following 21 or more: 416 times, 45x more than non-followers. Feed-to-tail conversion jumps from 44% for non-followers to around 50% at the first follow, then holds roughly steady. Every additional follow drives more visits; the conversion rate barely moves.

Posting scales similarly. Users following 21 or more posted to the feed 19 times per user over the window vs 0.09 for non-followers. Content supply and follow depth move together.

8Methodology

Cohort construction, data sources, metric definitions

Cohort (stitched)

Aug 17 to 24 (Phase 1 A/B): Treatment = TOAD_SOCIAL_FEED_ENABLE_PROFILE = on; Holdout = control. Aug 24 to Sep 14 (holdout window): Treatment = all three phase flags on; Holdout = holdout/control value on Phase 1 flag (confirmed from Amplitude user_properties). iOS and Android only (amplitude_project = 'amplitude_sbk').

Metric definitions

Community Feed opens per community user: Social Feed Page Viewed totals divided by _active unique users per day (logged_in, native, sportsbook)
Community Feed open session rate: sessions with at least 1 Social Feed Page Viewed divided by qualifying active sessions, daily
Feed-to-tail CVR: ordered funnel within a 30-minute session (Session ID held constant): Social Feed Page Viewed then Selection Success (Selection Page Name Source = social or social-feed). Holdout window avg ~47 to 48% both groups.
Tails per community user: PROPSUM(Bet Count on social Selection Success) divided by UNIQUES(Social Feed Page Viewed) that day. Both treatment and holdout shown.
Tails by source: share of all social tails by Tab Source (community / following / profile), scoped to users who followed 1+ person. Phase 1 ~94.5% community / 5.1% following. Holdout end ~86.7% community / 9.0% following.
Community-sourced handle: Selection Stake PROPSUM on social-sourced Selection Success, per community user
Follow depth charts: users bucketed by total Social Follow Action Selected (Interaction Type = followed) events during the holdout window. Cross-sectional: each bucket shows the pooled Aug 24 to Sep 14 average.
% of bets posted to feed: funnel Bet Success to Social Post Successful (Bet ID constant), daily OVER_TIME
Handle per social user (guardrail): Selection Stake PROPSUM divided by _active uniques, daily average
Active Session Rate (guardrail): sessions with at least 1 Bet Success divided by qualifying active sessions (logged_in, native, at least 5s), daily (%)

Notes

Phase 1 A/B window (Aug 17 to 24) is shaded on all time-series charts. Phase annotation lines are dashed vertical lines recessed behind data series. Treatment-only charts have no holdout line since holdout users cannot follow or view profiles. Sessions per social user y-axis runs 0 to 15. All other y-axes start at 0. Daily grain throughout. Cross-sectional charts pool the full Aug 24 to Sep 14 holdout window. Spikes in the holdout series reflect small sample size variance. None of the figures in this readout are adjusted for outliers or tested for statistical significance. This is a descriptive analysis.
